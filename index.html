// HOW IT WORKS

        
The box watches how you solve — then rebuilds itself.

        
    
          
  1. 01 · OBSERVEEvery room is tracked. Attempts, time spent, hints requested and which hint levels you needed.
    2. 
          
  2. 02 · ADAPT THE CLUEHints change shape. Nudge → Direction → Guided reasoning. Miss twice and the box offers a clearer clue; miss three times and it switches format — visual, step-by-step, a table. It never just gives you the answer.
    3. 
          
  3. 03 · CHOOSE THE PATHThe next room is picked for you. A fast, clean solve unlocks Challenge Mode. Needing support leads to Guided Discovery, which builds up gradually. Both paths reach the same core.
    4. 
          
  4. 04 · EXPLAINNothing is hidden. Every adaptation shows its reason, and the rules are inspectable in the Adaptive System panel.
    5. 
        

        
GlitchBox uses transparent, rule-based logic — not a trained AI model or external service. Insights describe one session of play, never ability or personality.
